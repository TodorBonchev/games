Hold jump to engage the jetpack, steer with movement controls, and release to descend. Land to recharge fuel. Frozen Orb sends out ice shards and ends in a burst; watch its button for the next cast.

Choose your weapon before deploying. Hold FIRE with any weapon; ammunition is unlimited. Clear waves to upgrade your build.

Follow the uplink arrow to the beacon. Keep nearby hostiles clear and stay inside for five seconds to earn armor, Overdrive, and bonus points. Uplinks are optional; leave the ring when you need breathing room.

Collect field supplies. Jump or leave the commander's warning ring before the slam lands.

Each encounter changes the horde. Runner Rush rewards movement; Iron Guard demands sustained fire. Spitters mark the ground before acid lands: leave the warning circle, then avoid the pool.

Chain eliminations within seven seconds to build your score bonus up to ×3. Taking a hit breaks momentum. Clear both islands to complete the operation, then continue in Endless if you want a tougher run.
