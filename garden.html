THE GARDENER’S FIELD GUIDE

A LITTLE
GREEN POWER.

  1. 01
    Grow your budget.

    Tap the glowing suns to collect 25 sun. Plant Sun blooms for a steady supply every 10 seconds.

  2. 02
    Build a team.

    Choose a plant, then tap an empty lawn tile. Pea pods shoot right along their lane. Shellnuts soak up bites.

  3. 03
    Protect every lane.

    Keep Sun blooms toward the left. Cover all five lanes with Pea pods before the bigger waves arrive.

  4. 04
    Clear your garden.

    Later gardens add runners, tougher armor, and up to five waves. Each lawnmower clears its lane once. After that, a zombie reaching your home ends the run. The shovel returns half a plant’s cost.
